# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Harassment, threats, discrimination, impersonation, unwanted tracking, doxxing and disclosure of private location data are prohibited. Discuss engineering decisions constructively and accept moderation decisions made to protect users.

Report concerns privately to the instance operator or repository maintainer through a configured private reporting channel. No public community or staffed moderation service is currently operated for this development project. Do not post personal/location details in public issues. Maintainers may remove harmful content and restrict access proportionately.
